Attention is the idea that made modern AI possible. Instead of reading a sentence one word at a time, a transformer lets every token look at every other token and decide what matters. The query asks what I am looking for. The key says what I contain. The value is what I hand over when someone attends to me. Multiply, scale, mask the future, softmax the rows, and take the weighted average. That is the whole trick, and it fits in ninety lines of NumPy.

Training a language model starts long before the model. Someone has to chop raw text into tokens, because a GPU cannot read letters. Byte pair encoding begins with 256 byte tokens and repeatedly merges the most frequent adjacent pair into a new one. The word the becomes one token after enough merges. Rare names shatter into three or four pieces. Numbers split unpredictably, which is why models are oddly bad at arithmetic. Token count decides your API bill, so every developer eventually learns to care.

A chat dataset for fine-tuning is one JSON object per line, with a list of messages. Each message has a role: system, user or assistant. Validate before you train. Look for duplicate prompts, empty content, roles that do not belong, and JSON that fails to parse. Split the file into train and validation sets with a fixed seed so the split is reproducible. The boring half of machine learning is the half that saves your money.

Backpropagation is just the chain rule applied carefully. Forward pass computes the prediction. The loss compares it with the truth. Then gradients flow backwards, layer by layer, each layer asking how much it contributed to the mistake. Gradient descent nudges every weight a little in the direction that reduces the loss. A learning rate too big makes the loss bounce around. Too small and training crawls. Every practitioner has watched a loss curve explode and muttered a bad word.

Regularization is the art of telling a model to stay humble. Dropout randomly silences neurons during training so no single path becomes a crutch. Weight decay shrinks parameters toward zero. Early stopping quits while the validation loss is still falling. Overfitting looks like a student who memorized last year's paper: perfect on practice, lost on the real exam.
